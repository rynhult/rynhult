Download

Rynhult is installed from its source release onto a Linux server. There is no separate desktop or phone app to download for v1.

Verify what you download

Download SHA256SUMS and its detached signature, SHA256SUMS.asc, with the release files. Follow release verification: compare the public key's full fingerprints, verify the signature, then check every listed SHA-256. Stop on a missing signature or failed check. Only SHA256SUMS is signed; the in-app updater does not verify signatures yet. The install guide explains the next steps.
